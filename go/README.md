# Sparkle Go: Pretend Play Jobs

An ad-free pretend play and dress-up game for kids on iPhone and iPad, made for children who can't read yet.

**[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6829414552)** · [finjet.ai](https://finjet.ai/en/)

- **No ads, no subscriptions.** Free to download; one optional one-time purchase (US$2.99) unlocks everything, with Family Sharing.
- **No reading needed.** There are no words anywhere in the game.
- **Pick a job and get dressed:** firefighter, police officer, astronaut, pilot, ferry captain, chef.
- **20+ missions:** rescue a kitten from a tree, fix a satellite, load a plane, decorate a cake and more.
- **Calm play:** no losing, no timers, nothing scary or violent.
- **Offline and private:** works without internet and collects no data.
- Ages 5 and under (App Store rating 4+). iPhone and iPad, iOS 17 or later.

Made by [finjet](https://finjet.ai/en/), an independent studio in Istanbul.
